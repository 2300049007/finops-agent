Compliance Overview

Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

The system enforces **Data Minimization** and **Purpose Limitation**. PII elements such as Aadhaar number (12 digits) and PAN (10 alphanumeric digits) are automatically masked in explanations and logs before storage.

RBI Grievance Directives

Payment failures and unauthorized transactions require a strict escalation SLA and precise logging of underlying error codes (`ERR_NETWORK_TIMEOUT`, etc.) to support downstream audits.
